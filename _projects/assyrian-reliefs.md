The palaces of Nimrud, Khorsabad and Nineveh were lined with carved stone reliefs, commissioned by successive Assyrian kings. We use deep learning to recognise the style associated with each king, to cluster and retrieve related scenes across museum collections, and to propose completions for damaged reliefs.
