The proto-cuneiform tablets from late fourth-millennium Uruk are among the earliest written records in the world. Most of them are accounts, but their type (grain, livestock, labour, and more) is often not recorded in their metadata. We train classifiers that assign account types from the content of each text, to enrich the metadata of the archaic corpus and to make it searchable.
