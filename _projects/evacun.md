EvaCun gives researchers a common test bed for cuneiform languages. The first edition (2023, at the ALT workshop in Macau) evaluated machine translation from Akkadian and Sumerian into English. The second (2025, at the ALP workshop at NAACL) added lemmatization and token prediction with large language models. A third edition is planned with ALP 2027. The lab organises the tasks, prepares the data and publishes an overview of the results.
