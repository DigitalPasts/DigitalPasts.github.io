ArchAIa is a six-month pilot of the Schmidt Sciences Humanities and AI Virtual Institute. It asks how foundation models can describe, compare and connect archaeological artefacts, from inscribed tablets to reliefs and pottery, across museum collections and excavation archives. The Digital Pasts Lab contributes its cuneiform image and text datasets and its experience with tablet shape and style analysis.
