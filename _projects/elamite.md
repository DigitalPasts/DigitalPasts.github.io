Elamite, the language of ancient south-western Iran, was written in cuneiform for more than two thousand years, but its digital documentation is still in its infancy. This project builds an Elamite lemma base and publishes it as linked open data, so that words can be traced across periods and connected to resources for Akkadian, Sumerian and Old Persian.
