Ancient writing survives on clay, stone and papyrus, in scripts that current AI models were never trained to read. This project builds multimodal foundation models that learn from images of inscriptions and their transcriptions together, so that one model can help read cuneiform, ancient North Arabian and other scripts. It brings together epigraphers and computer scientists from Israel and the United States, and runs from 2027 to 2029 with funding from the Schmidt Sciences Humanities and AI Virtual Institute (HAVI).
