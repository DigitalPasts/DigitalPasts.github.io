Hittite is the oldest attested Indo-European language, written in cuneiform in Anatolia in the second millennium BCE. This project turns published Hittite texts, with their glosses and German translations, into a machine-readable corpus. The data supports language models for Hittite and stylometric studies of Hittite scribes, genres and orthography.
