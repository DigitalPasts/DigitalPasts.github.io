Our field

Ancient Language Processing

Ancient Language Processing (ALP) is the branch of natural language processing for ancient languages and scripts. Scarce data, broken texts and writing systems that standard tools cannot handle call for their own methods. We build models, datasets and shared tasks for Akkadian, Sumerian, Hittite, Elamite and other ancient languages, and co-organise the ALP and ML4AL workshops at major NLP conferences.
